Security & Privacy

Secure by Design

TallyTrigger is being built with strong encryption standards for storing merchant authorization credentials. Security is a core design requirement, not an afterthought.

No Card Data Involved

TallyTrigger works with sales reporting data only. Raw payment card data remains within Clover's own infrastructure — we don't request or receive it.

OAuth-Based Access

TallyTrigger connects to Clover using their official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow — no POS password required. You stay in control and can revoke access at any time.

Our goal is to handle your data minimally and responsibly — processing reports without permanently storing raw transaction data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

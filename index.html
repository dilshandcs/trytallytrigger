Clover® POS Sales Reports

A reporting utility being built for Clover® POS merchants — designed to automatically retrieve your sales data, format it into your preferred columns, and deliver the report (in XLSX, PDF, CSV or IIF format) to your accountant, bookkeeper, or team on a schedule you define, e.g. every Monday at 6 AM.

Fully Automated

Designed to configure once and run silently. A background scheduler will fetch, format, and email your reports — daily, weekly, or monthly — without any manual steps.

Custom Ledger Mapping

Planned to let you rename any data column to match your accountant's exact template — with the ability to route different report types to different recipients simultaneously.

Multi-Format Export

Planned output formats: ledger-ready .XLSX, printable .PDF, flat .CSV, and QuickBooks-compatible .IIF. Clean decimals and readable dates — no manual cleanup.

QuickBooks Live Sync

Coming Later

A planned Pro tier feature. Push every Clover sale directly into QuickBooks Online in real time — no email, no manual import. Available in a future release after beta.

Security & Privacy

Secure by Design

TallyTrigger is being built with strong encryption standards for storing merchant authorization credentials. Security is a core design requirement, not an afterthought.

No Card Data Involved

TallyTrigger works with sales reporting data only. Raw payment card data remains within Clover's own infrastructure — we don't request or receive it.

OAuth-Based Access

TallyTrigger connects to Clover using their official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow — no POS password required. You stay in control and can revoke access at any time.

Our goal is to handle your data minimally and responsibly — processing reports without permanently storing raw transaction data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

TallyTrigger ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and safeguard information when you visit our pre-launch website or submit your email address to register interest in early access. The application itself is not yet available.

This service is currently operated by an independent developer. No registered business entity has been formed at this time. This policy may be updated if the project becomes operated by a registered business entity.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services:

  • Early Access Contact Information: When you submit the interest form, we collect your email address and any optional feature requests you choose to share. This is the only data collected at this pre-launch stage.
  • Merchant Data (Future App Usage): If the application launches, we intend to process authorized point-of-sale data retrieved through Clover APIs solely to generate requested reports. No such data is collected at this pre-launch stage.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data primarily to:

  • Process early-access interest registrations and send updates regarding product availability.
  • If the application launches, deliver scheduled automated report emails to recipients you designate during setup.
  • Provide technical support and address feedback from registered interest submissions.
We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize merchant data or personal email addresses to third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security

We are designing TallyTrigger with security as a core requirement. Before the application launches, appropriate technical and organizational safeguards will be implemented to protect customer information.

5. Data Retention

  • Transaction Data: Our intent is to process Clover order data only as needed to generate your report, without long-term storage of raw transaction records. Exact retention practices will be confirmed before launch.
  • Account Data: Information needed to operate your account (schedule settings, delivery logs) will be retained while your account is active.
  • Beta Waitlist Emails: Email addresses collected during pre-launch are used only for product updates and will be deleted upon request or within 12 months if the product does not launch.

6. Your Rights & Opt-Out

You may contact us at any time to exercise the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion (Right to Erasure): Request deletion of your email address and any data we hold about you. We will remove your information within 30 days of a verified request.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from beta or product communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly.

7. QuickBooks Integration Data (Pro Tier)

A QuickBooks Online integration is planned as a Pro tier feature. Specific data handling practices for this integration will be documented here before it is made available. The integration will use Intuit's official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow and will not request access beyond what is needed to deliver transaction data to your QuickBooks company file.

9. Third-Party Service Providers

If the application launches, we expect to use service providers such as:

  • Application Hosting: Application infrastructure and database hosting
  • Email Delivery: Transactional email delivery for reports and notifications
  • File Storage: Encrypted cloud object storage for generated report files
  • Billing: If paid plans become available, payment processing is expected to be handled by Stripe.
  • Clover API: Clover Network, LLC — for merchant data access under your authorized OAuth grant
  • QuickBooks API (Planned — Pro tier, future release): Intuit Inc. — for transaction delivery under your authorized OAuth grant. This integration is not active in beta. Data handling details will be updated here before it is made available.

2. Service Description

TallyTrigger is an automated reporting utility for Clover® POS merchants. The service is being developed in phases:

Beta (Initial Release):

  • Scheduled Report Delivery: Automated retrieval of your Clover sales data and scheduled email delivery to one or more recipients at a frequency you configure.
  • Multi-Format Export: Reports formatted as Excel (.XLSX), PDF, CSV, or QuickBooks Desktop import file (.IIF).
  • Custom Column Mapping: Rename data fields to match your accountant's ledger naming conventions.
  • Delivery Log: A history of all past report delivery attempts accessible in your dashboard.

Planned Future Release (Pro Tier):

  • QuickBooks Online Live Sync: Real-time push of Clover transaction data directly into your QuickBooks Online company file. This feature is not available in beta and will be introduced in a later release.

Features and availability may change. We will notify active subscribers of material changes to the service.
